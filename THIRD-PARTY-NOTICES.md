resources, exact upstream/local hashes, adaptations and retained license/NOTICE files are owned and distributed by the
separate `agent-skills` source and its closed plugin payloads. Inspect that source and the actual installed payload;
a project selection manifest is not evidence of installed file hashes or a replacement for required notices.

This template references official tool/framework documentation and creates original scaffolding/project guidance.
It does not redistribute BattleBang, ESP or Unitree applications or grant their runtime/configuration rights.
No blanket license has yet been selected for original template/shared-source material. Before public publication,
choose the appropriate license and audit imported obligations, secrets and proprietary/team scope. Do not label all
shared content MIT: upstream terms vary and remain with their payloads.

Generated projects choose their own product license. Generation does not grant publication clearance or prove that an
installed plugin matches a recorded source revision.
